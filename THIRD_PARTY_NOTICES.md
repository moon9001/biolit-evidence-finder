# Third party notices

No third-party library source or binary is vendored in this release. Original
license notices and lock-file license fields remain unchanged. Installed packages
retain their own copyright and license files; preserve those when redistributing
dependencies. The prototype MIT license does not replace a dependency license.

The replay environment records PyMuPDF, SQLAlchemy, pydantic, pydantic-settings,
FastAPI, httpx, python-multipart, python-dotenv, Pillow and NumPy. The optional
interface also uses uvicorn and pytesseract. Consult the licenses included with
the exact installed versions; in particular PyMuPDF has separate licensing terms.
This inventory reports lock metadata and is not a new license grant.
